Iron-man

CEO of Stark Industries

Iron Man is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Co-created by writer and editor Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Tales of Suspense #39 in 1962 (cover dated March 1963) and received his own title with Iron Man #1 in 1968. Shortly after his creation, Iron Man became a founding member of the superhero team the Avengers, alongside Thor, Ant-Man, the Wasp, and the Hulk. Iron Man stories, individually and with the Avengers, have been published consistently since the character's creation

Genius, Billionaire, Playboy, Philanthropist

He is revered for his intellect, graduating from MIT at 17 and developing advanced AI (JARVIS, FRIDAY) and numerous specialized armors.

Projects

Iron Man Armor: Mark I

The Iron Man Armor: Mark I was Tony Stark's first Iron Man suit, built to help him escape from forced captivity by the Ten Rings. The suit was crafted from the limited resources around him, including salvaged pieces of scrap metal, machine parts, and pieces of Jericho missiles.

Iron Man Armor: Mark III

The Iron Man Armor: Mark III was Tony Stark's third Iron Man suit and an upgrade from the Mark II, improving on previous flaws, in particular an icing problem that Stark solved using a more cold-resistant alloy. It was the first suit to integrate an advanced on-board weapons system and was the first to adopt the classic red and gold color scheme. It was notably worn during the Battle of Gulmira and the Duel of Los Angeles.

Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIV (Hulkbuster)

The Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIV, better known as Hulkbuster, was Tony Stark's forty-fourth Iron Man suit, made with the help of Bruce Banner. Created solely for the purpose of restraining Hulk, it is a modular suit that Stark donned while already in another armor, such as the Mark XLIII. It was launched from an orbital tracking platform known as Veronica. It was used by Stark to fight Hulk in Johannesburg.
