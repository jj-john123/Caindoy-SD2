Iron-man

CEO of Stark Industries

Iron Man is a superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Co-created by writer and editor Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Tales of Suspense #39 in 1962 (cover dated March 1963) and received his own title with Iron Man #1 in 1968. Shortly after his creation, Iron Man became a founding member of the superhero team the Avengers, alongside Thor, Ant-Man, the Wasp, and the Hulk. Iron Man stories, individually and with the Avengers, have been published consistently since the character's creation

Genius, Billionaire, Playboy, Philanthropist

He is revered for his intellect, graduating from MIT at 17 and developing advanced AI (JARVIS, FRIDAY) and numerous specialized armors.

Projects

Iron Man Armor: Mark I

The Iron Man Armor: Mark I was Tony Stark's first Iron Man suit, built to help him escape from forced captivity by the Ten Rings. The suit was crafted from the limited resources around him, including salvaged pieces of scrap metal, machine parts, and pieces of Jericho missiles.

Iron Man Armor: Mark III

The Iron Man Armor: Mark III was Tony Stark's third Iron Man suit and an upgrade from the Mark II, improving on previous flaws, it was the first suit to integrate an advanced on-board weapons system and was the first to adopt the classic red and gold color scheme.

Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIV (Hulkbuster)

The Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIV, better known as Hulkbuster, was Tony Stark's forty-fourth Iron Man suit, made with the help of Bruce Banner. Created solely for the purpose of restraining Hulk, it was launched from an orbital tracking platform known as Veronica. It was used by Stark to fight Hulk in Johannesburg.

Iron Man Armor: Mark L

The Iron Man Armor: Mark L is Tony Stark's fiftieth Iron Man suit. Upon creating the second Hulkbuster and Rescue armor, Stark created the Mark L to replace the Mark XLVII. This armor was more advanced than any of his preceding armors as it utilizes nanotechnology. It was used during the Infinity War, most notably to fight Thanos on Titan.

Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIX (Rescue Armor)

The Iron Man Armor: Mark XLIX,[2] also known as the Rescue Armor, is an armor used by Pepper Potts. It was designed by Tony Stark, and bears a strong resemblance to the Iron Man Armor: Mark LXXXV. Potts used it during the Battle of Earth to fight Thanos' army alongside her husband, the Avengers and their allies.

Iron Man Armor: Mark LXXXV (Final Armor)

The Iron Man Armor: Mark LXXXV is Tony Stark's eighty-fifth and final Iron Man suit. An upgrade to the Mark L armor, it was used by Stark during the Time Heist and the Battle of Earth.
